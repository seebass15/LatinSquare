📖 How to Play Latin Square

The Core Rule

A Latin Square is an n × n grid filled with numbers, such that each number appears exactly once in each row and exactly once in each column.

🎮 Arcade Mode (Survival)

  • Dynamic Tray: Numbers appear in a rotating candidate batch. Placed numbers are spent from your tray until the batch refreshes.
  • Cell Aging: Every turn, cells age and harden. Watch out for locking blocks!
  • Survival Goal: Keep clearing rows and columns to stay alive and rack up high scores.

🧩 Classic Mode (Puzzle Solver)

  • Guaranteed Solvable: Every puzzle starts with randomized pre-filled clues and is mathematically guaranteed to have a solution.
  • Permanent Toolbar: Candidates act as a permanent toolbar—select and stamp numbers as many times as you need.
  • Logic Goal: Fill out the entire grid completely without breaking row and column uniqueness!
