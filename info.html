📖 How to Play Latin Square

The Core Rule

A Latin Square is an n × n grid filled with $n$ different numbers, such that each number appears exactly once in each row and exactly once in each column (similar to Sudoku's uniqueness rule).

🎮 Arcade Mode (Survival)

  • Dynamic Tray: Numbers appear in a rotating candidate batch. When you place a number, it's spent from your tray until the batch is empty and refreshes.
  • Cell Aging: Every turn, cells age and their durability changes. Watch out for hardening blocks!
  • Survival Goal: Keep clearing rows and columns to stay alive and rack up high scores before the board locks up.

🧩 Classic Mode (Puzzle Solver)

  • Guaranteed Solvable: Every puzzle starts with randomized pre-filled clues and is mathematically guaranteed to have a solution.
  • Permanent Toolbar: Your candidates (`1` to `n`) act as a permanent toolbar—you can select and stamp numbers as many times as you need.
  • Logic Goal: Fill out the entire grid completely without breaking row and column uniqueness!
